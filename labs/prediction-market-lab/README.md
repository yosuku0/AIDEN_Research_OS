# Prediction Market Lab

## Purpose

Research lab for analyzing prediction market protocols, oracle systems, resolution mechanisms, and market efficiency. Documentation-only research artifact.

## Boundary

Research scaffold only. No execution, signing, or production-write authority. All outputs are governance artifacts.

## Research Questions

1. How do different oracle systems affect market resolution accuracy?
2. What prediction market mechanisms achieve highest efficiency?
3. How do resolution delays impact market participation?
4. What is the relationship between liquidity and prediction accuracy?

## Methodology

- Oracle system comparison
- Resolution mechanism analysis
- Market efficiency simulation
- Participation pattern study

## Schema

Uses `schemas/research-lab/`:
- `simulation-result.schema.json` - Market simulations
- `evidence-bundle.schema.json` - Research evidence
- `audit-event.schema.json` - Activity tracking
